About Jerilyn

This Was My Calling.
Not My Plan.

Jerilyn Strong is a first-generation American — the daughter and granddaughter of Filipino immigrants, and the descendant of Dr. James Walter Strong, a Spanish-American War veteran whose legacy lives on in a boulevard in the Philippines.

She started her first business at 12 years old — a car wash on her mother's driveway, her baby cousins as her first team, paid in ice cream money. By her early twenties she had generated her first million. By 25 years in, she had helped clients save over $863M in taxes.

She is a scientist who teaches the science of money. A former IRS auditor who learned the system from the inside. A warrior who shows up, tells the truth, and does not wait for permission.

  • Financial Behavior Coach — Science-Based, Evidence-Backed
  • Enrolled Agent (EA) · Former IRS Auditor
  • CFO & Board Advisor to 62+ companies worldwide
