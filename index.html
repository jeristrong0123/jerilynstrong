About Jerilyn

"When there is a will, there is a way."
My grandmother taught me that.

Jerilyn Strong is an Enrolled Agent, former IRS Auditor, and Master's in Finance — with 27 years of experience helping entrepreneurs build the structures that protect everything they have worked to create.

She started solving tax problems at her mother's kitchen table at age 14. Today she serves as a Interim CFO and Board Advisor to companies across the country — and speaks on stages that move entrepreneurs from survival to legacy.

Through her work with Better Bridge and financial literacy communities, Jerilyn believes that financial imprisonment is just as real as any other kind — and just as solvable.
